Detailed User Guide

Complete operating workflow for shop owners, managers, cashiers, and platform administrators.

Version 1.0 · July 2026 · No images included

1. Start here

Stockora is a multi-shop POS and inventory system. Your work area depends on the account used at sign-in: a shop account manages one shop; a platform administrator manages all shops and subscriptions.

First-time shop setup

  1. Sign in at the Stockora login page, or register a new shop if registration is enabled.
  2. Open Settings. Enter the shop name, address, phone, email, invoice details, and other business information. Save and confirm the visible shop name is correct.
  3. Create product Categories.
  4. Add Suppliers before receiving supplier stock.
  5. Add products manually, or download the import template and use Import for a large catalogue.
  6. Verify product price, cost, starting quantity, unit, barcode (when used), and low-stock level before the first sale.
Before going live: test one small sale and inspect the generated receipt, stock quantity, and Sales record. Do not share a password; use individual accounts wherever the installation supports them.

2. Catalogue and stock workflow

A. Create a clean catalogue

Use Categories for sensible groups such as Grocery, Drinks, Mobile Accessories, or Stationery. In Products, create one record per sellable item. Use a distinct name and barcode so that POS search finds the correct item quickly. Cost price supports profit reporting; selling price is the normal POS price. Mark discontinued items inactive instead of creating confusing duplicates.

B. Receive stock from a supplier

  1. Open Suppliers and confirm the supplier exists and is active. Add contact data and opening balance where appropriate.
  2. Open Purchases and create the supplier invoice or purchase entry.
  3. Select the product(s), received quantity, unit cost, purchase date, invoice number, amount paid, and notes.
  4. Save the purchase. Confirm the stock quantity rises and the purchase/invoice appears in its history.
  5. If money remains outstanding, record later settlement through the supplier-payment workflow rather than changing the original purchase total.

3. POS sale workflow

Standard retail sale

  1. Open POS Billing. Use the search field, category filters, or a barcode scanner to find products.
  2. Select each product. Check the cart quantity and make sure it does not exceed available stock.
  3. For a named customer, select or create the customer. For wholesale, switch the sale type and select the bulk buyer when applicable.
  4. Apply only an approved discount. Review subtotal, discount, taxes/charges shown by the installation, and final total.
  5. Select payment method: cash, card, online, or credit. Set paid, partial, or credit status accurately.
  6. Complete the sale only after the amount and cart are correct. The sale reduces stock and creates a sales record.
  7. Print or open the invoice/receipt and hand it to the customer. Search the Sales list later by date or invoice number if it must be reprinted.

Credit and wholesale

Use Customers for retail customer details and history. Use Bulk Buyers for wholesale customers, their limits, and appropriate wholesale pricing/discount policy. A credit or partial transaction must be recorded with the real balance; do not mark it fully paid merely to close the POS screen.

  1. Open Credit Dues after making a credit/partial sale.
  2. Locate the customer/buyer and confirm the outstanding amount against the sale.
  3. When payment is received, record the amount, date, payment method, and reference. Keep the receipt/reference provided by the customer.
  4. Recheck the balance. A payment should reduce the due; it should not be entered as a new sale.

Returns

  1. Open Customer Returns, locate the original sale, and verify the eligible items and quantities.
  2. Select only returned items, write the reason, and choose the appropriate refund/return handling available on the screen.
  3. Save the return and verify the linked history and stock movement. Returned sellable stock should be handled through this process, not by editing a product quantity without explanation.

4. End-of-day and reporting workflow

Recommended daily close

  1. Finish outstanding POS transactions; do not leave an incorrect cart open as a substitute for a pending sale.
  2. Count cash and compare it with completed cash sales. Investigate differences before closing the day.
  3. Review Sales for cancelled, partial, credit, and unusually discounted transactions.
  4. Review Credit Dues, Reorder Alerts, and new online orders.
  5. Open Z Report for the day’s operational totals and retain it with the cash/shift records.
  6. Use Export to create a secure backup of the required records. Verify its date range before sending it to accounting.

5. Subscription and online-store workflow

Subscription

Open Subscription to see plan status, dates, and renewal details. Core shop access is blocked when there is no active subscription or it has expired, except for the allowed renewal/settings area. A Free Trial provides core POS access but does not unlock premium features. For a paid subscription, retain payment references and confirm the plan/end date after renewal.

Premium Commerce Cloud tools

Paid active subscriptions unlock the premium tools. The normal workflow is:

  1. Open Store Wizard to create the storefront baseline.
  2. Choose a design in Theme Marketplace.
  3. Use Store Customizer to apply shop branding, store settings, and presentation choices.
  4. Open Commerce Cloud to manage the online-selling setup and public-store details.
  5. Review Online Orders regularly. Confirm availability, update the order state according to the screen’s workflow, and fulfil it through your agreed delivery process.
  6. Use AI Lab only as a decision-support tool; verify its suggestions against actual stock, prices, and business policy.
Important: premium pages can be locked by subscription status. Renew through the subscription process; do not rely on a saved page URL to bypass access controls.

6. Platform administrator workflow

Platform administration is separate from the shop workspace. Sign in using an administrator account and use the Admin menu.

  1. Open Shops to create, inspect, activate, suspend, or manage shop records. Confirm owner contact data before changing status.
  2. Use Plans to maintain offered subscription plans, price, duration, and availability.
  3. Use Subscriptions and Subscription Calendar to create/renew subscriptions and monitor end dates.
  4. Record subscription payments in Payments with the correct shop, amount, method, date, status, and reference number.
  5. Generate a receipt in Invoice Generator from a completed payment. Verify the shop and amount before printing.
  6. Use Expiry Alerts, Revenue, Analytics, Top Shops, Feature Usage, and Platform Health to monitor the platform.
  7. Publish time-sensitive information with Announcements, then check the intended audience and message wording.
  8. Use Activity Log when investigating a business or platform event.

Problem checklist

  • Cannot sign in: confirm account type, email, password, and active account status.
  • Cannot use a shop page: check active subscription dates and shop status.
  • Product missing in POS: confirm active product status, shop ownership, and available stock.
  • Totals do not match: check filters, payment status, returns, credit payments, and date range.
  • Import fails: use the download template, verify required columns, and remove malformed rows.
